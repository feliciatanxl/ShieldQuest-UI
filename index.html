ShieldQuest is a pixel-art adventure set in a Singapore HDB neighbourhood. Youths explore, investigate and make the call, practising what to do when a friend asks them to cross the line.

Why we're building this

Knowing it's wrong isn't the hard part.

Making the right call when friends are watching is.

4,109 shop theft cases recorded in 2025

One of the top offences among youths arrested.

Source: Singapore Police Force, Annual Crime Brief 2025

Peer pressure, not ignorance

Youths often get pulled in as the lookout or the friend who "just watched". That is still involvement.

One-off talks fade

Most crime-prevention messages are a single assembly. Skills stick when youths practise them.

What makes ShieldQuest different

Learning by choosing, not by listening.

Play, not a lecture

Every decision is the lesson. No slides, no quiz sheets.

Set where youths live

Void decks, minimarts and bus stops they pass every day.

Facts before assumptions

Players learn to separate what they saw from what they guessed.

Safe, not vigilante

No confronting or accusing. Youths set a boundary and get trusted help.

Measured skill by skill

Each S.H.I.E.L.D. skill is scored on its own, before and after play.

Better together soon

Co-op missions where sharing clues is the only way through.

How it works

The S.H.I.E.L.D. framework

Six skills. Each one practised and scored.

S

Spot the Risk

Notice the facts. Don't jump to conclusions.

H

Hold Before Acting

Pause and set a safe boundary.

I

Identify the Influence

Name the pressure, and the role you're being pushed into.

E

Evaluate Consequences

See the fallout now and later.

L

Lead the Right Choice

Steer a friend somewhere safer, respectfully.

D

Defend Your Community

Get the right help with clear, honest facts.

How we help youths

Skills they can use outside the game.

We test with fresh scenarios before and after play, so we measure real understanding, not memorised answers.
